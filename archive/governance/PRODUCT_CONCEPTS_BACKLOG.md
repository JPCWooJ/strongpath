# Entry 1 — Family-Networked Accountability App

**Status:** Captured. Phase 2 candidate. Not in active development.

**Origin:** Brand Ambassador session 2, April 18, 2026. Surfaced during persona reality-check conversation for the Proactive Caregiver. Founder-originated.

**Related persona:** Proactive Caregiver ("David") primary; Pre-Frail Adult ("Margaret") secondary. See PERSONAS.md.

**Connects to prior work:** The 2019–2023 StrongPath digital therapeutics company built a related capability using Trainerize as a white-label engine — aimed at healthcare providers (B2B). The concept below pivots that architecture to families (B2C).

## The Concept

A subscription app where multiple family members train together on the StrongPath framework, with the family itself as the adherence mechanism.

Core mechanic: the parent (often 75–90, often low baseline, often resistant to independent adherence) is a user. The adult child (often 45–60, often already concerned about their own aging trajectory) is also a user. Both follow age- and fitness-appropriate versions of the StrongPath protocol. Each can see the other's training activity. Each can send encouragement, nudges, or check-in messages when the other has not moved. An AI agent supplements human contact — generating warm, personalized nudges on behalf of a busy adult child, or providing a conversational training companion for the parent on days family contact is not available.

The product is not a tracker. It is a small, warm, high-trust loop of people who care about each other, held together by a training protocol and assisted by AI.

## Why This Is a Real Opportunity, Not a Feature

- **Solves a pain point no one else is solving.** Fitness apps treat family as a marketing channel ("refer a friend"). This treats family as the core adherence architecture. The pain point — "I gave my parent the book and they didn't follow through" — is universal in the caregiver persona and has no current good answer.
- **The caregiver is a willing subscriber.** The Proactive Caregiver persona has the means and the motivation. They will pay for adherence on behalf of a parent. They are paying for peace of mind as much as for the protocol.
- **The caregiver is also a user.** The multi-generational loop works in both directions. The caregiver nudges the parent. The parent's progress motivates the caregiver. A parent who sees their child training is more likely to train themselves. The adherence mechanism is bidirectional by design, and that is what makes this stronger than a unidirectional caregiver-to-parent app.
- **LTV economics are different.** An affiliate book buyer is a $5–20 transaction. A digital program buyer is $49–129 one-time. A family app subscriber is $20–50/month, with the product becoming more valuable the longer the family stays engaged. The LTV math changes the business.
- **Evidence and authority compound.** Every family that uses the product generates training data, adherence data, and — with consent — outcomes. Over time this becomes a research asset that reinforces the StrongPath authority position and potentially supports clinical partnerships.

## The Minimum Viable Version

- Two-to-three family members per household account, each with their own training profile.
- Age- and baseline-appropriate StrongPath protocols available at multiple starting points.
- Shared family feed: who trained today, who did not.
- Low-friction encouragement: pre-written warm messages the caregiver can send with one tap.
- An AI companion that can nudge on the caregiver's behalf when needed, trained in the StrongPath voice and compliance guardrails (see BRAND.md section 9.5).
- Weekly family summary: what each member accomplished, what is next.

## What It Is Not

- Not a medical device. No diagnostic, no treatment claims.
- Not a replacement for a physical therapist. Clear handoffs when clinical support is needed.
- Not a passive tracker. Passive tracking without family layer exists already and is commodity.
- Not a Phase 1 product. The build is substantial. Required prerequisites include: validated content library, basic membership infrastructure, brand in market, and capital.

## Open Questions for Phase 2 Product Workstream

- Build, partner, or white-label? Trainerize worked once for the prior company. Is there a better 2026-era option?
- What is the right price point? $20/month per family? Tiered by household size?
- Who owns the family account? The caregiver (default) or the parent (independence signal)?
- How does the AI agent avoid stepping on the warmth of the human relationship? The product fails if family members substitute AI nudges for real contact.
- What is the minimum viable clinical advisor presence needed to ship? This connects to the Phase 2 medical advisor hire flagged in BRAND.md and PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- Privacy and consent: family members can see each other's training. How granular? What can be hidden? What regulatory framework (HIPAA-adjacent, state privacy laws) applies?

## Rough Complexity Assessment

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **Engineering** | Substantial. Likely 6–12 months from decision to ship, depending on build-vs-partner choice. Not a weekend project. |
| **Content** | Moderate. Program content is largely in place through the book and initial digital products; needs adaptation to app-delivered format. |
| **Compliance** | Non-trivial. Health data, family-shared data, and the AI agent each add compliance surface area. Legal review required before shipping. |
| **Capital** | Meaningful. Beyond Phase 1 bootstrap cap. Likely requires either revenue from Phase 1 digital products at scale or a specific raise against this product. |
| **Brand fit** | High. Extends StrongPath from a content brand to a product brand without violating the book/product firewall (per BRAND.md section 9.1). Claims continue to attach to the book; the product is infrastructure. |

## Recommended Decision Now

Document and hold. Do not build. Do not announce. Do not seed demand in Phase 1 content — we do not sell a product that does not exist.
