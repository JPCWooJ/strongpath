## Register

brand

## Users

StrongPath serves adults after 50 who want to stay strong, active, and independent, and adult children or family members helping aging parents act earlier.

## Product Purpose

StrongPath is an evidence-based editorial health platform that helps readers understand muscle loss, strength training, protein, recovery, and practical next steps without hype or wellness-funnel behavior.

## Brand Personality

Calm, editorial, practical, and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Do not make StrongPath look like SaaS, influencer wellness, supplement marketing, aggressive ecommerce, pharmaceutical advertising, generic startup design, or decorative design for its own sake.

## Design Principles

- Editorial trust before commercial pressure.
- Typography, hierarchy, and spacing carry the experience.
- One dominant action per section.
- Book and evidence cues are authority objects, not decoration.
- Mobile readability and tap comfort are required for public pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve readable body sizes, clear contrast, semantic structure, reduced-motion safety, and mobile-first legibility for older adults and families.

## Codex Usage

Use Impeccable-style review for UI/UX, layout, spacing, visual rhythm, typography, critique, polish, and frontend anti-pattern detection.

Do not invoke Impeccable for backend, governance, content-only, infrastructure, CMS, metadata, analytics, deployment, or non-UI tasks unless the user explicitly asks.
