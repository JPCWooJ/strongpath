# EDITORIAL_WORKFLOW.md

## Purpose

Canonical Layer 2 end-to-end editorial operating workflow for StrongPath.

This file defines how articles move from topic idea to published, refreshed, deferred, or archived state. It connects topic selection, keyword intake, research, briefs, drafting, review, QA, scoring, founder gates, revision loops, publishing readiness, updates, and deprecation.

This is an editorial governance document. It does not define CMS implementation architecture, sprint-management systems, analytics/reporting systems, enterprise newsroom bureaucracy, or AI automation architecture.

## 1. Core Principle

StrongPath editorial workflow exists to reduce chaos and protect quality.

The workflow should make it clear:

- what state an article is in
- who owns the next action
- what evidence is required
- what must be reviewed
- when founder judgment is needed
- when an article is ready, deferred, or archived

Do not create process for its own sake. Use the lightest workflow that preserves trust.

## 2. Article Lifecycle Stages

Canonical lifecycle:

1. Idea
2. Candidate Topic
3. Approved Topic
4. Research Needed
5. Research Packet Ready
6. Brief Ready
7. Drafting
8. Editorial Review
9. QA Review
10. Scoring
11. Founder Review, when required
12. Revision
13. Publish Ready
14. Published
15. Refresh Needed
16. Deprecated or Archived

An article has one primary state at a time.

## 3. Topic Selection Workflow

Topic selection starts with reader need, not volume.

A topic may enter the workflow from:

- `keyword-universe.md`
- a publishing-plan priority
- a persona need
- a research opportunity
- a founder directive
- an authority gap
- a refresh or merge need

Approve a topic only when it has:

- clear reader
- clear search or editorial intent
- clear role: pillar, satellite, FAQ, book/authority page, product/support page, or tool/support asset
- safe claim territory
- plausible evidence support
- internal-linking role

Reject or defer topics that depend on unsafe claims, weak evidence, crisis intent, or premature commerce.

## 4. Keyword Intake Workflow

Keyword intake follows `SEO_SYSTEMS.md`.

For each keyword or cluster, record:

- primary keyword or phrase
- secondary terms
- search intent
- persona and moment
- pillar or satellite role
- claim-risk level
- excluded or caution terms
- commerce sensitivity
- recommended action: target, narrow, merge, defer, reject

Search demand does not approve the topic. It informs the doorway.

## 5. Research Workflow Integration

Research follows `RESEARCH_SYSTEMS.md`.

Research is required before drafting when:

- article makes health, safety, mechanism, intervention, or quantitative claims
- topic is pillar-level
- topic is commerce-sensitive
- article touches supplements, fall risk, frailty, hospitalization, surgery, contraindications, or deconditioned adults
- article needs current consensus

Research handoff must include:

- approved claims
- unsupported claims
- source list
- citation classes
- source freshness
- safety notes
- commerce-sensitive boundaries

Do not draft from raw links.

## 6. Article Brief Workflow

The brief turns topic and research into writing direction.

Minimum brief:

- working title
- content type
- primary persona
- search intent
- target keyword or phrase
- article role in cluster
- required claims
- do-not-claim list
- required sources or research packet
- internal-link targets
- CTA direction
- brand/tone notes
- commerce or affiliate boundaries
- founder questions, if any

The brief is a contract. Deviate only when the reason is explicit.

## 7. Drafting Workflow

Drafting starts only when the topic, brief, and research requirements are clear.

Drafting rules:

- answer the reader's intent early
- keep claims within approved evidence
- cite or mark claims needing citation
- preserve persona language
- follow brand voice
- keep commerce after education
- include planned internal links
- surface safety limits when relevant

If a new unsupported claim appears during drafting, mark it for research or cut it.

## 8. Editorial Review Workflow

Editorial review checks the article before formal QA.

Review for:

- structure
- clarity
- reader fit
- argument flow
- completeness for the article type
- overlong or underdeveloped sections
- unsupported expansions beyond the brief
- obvious brand or persona drift

Editorial review may return the article to drafting before QA.

## 9. QA Workflow Integration

QA follows `CONTENT_QA.md`.

QA checks:

- claim verification
- citation verification
- persona alignment
- brand/tone
- commerce firewall
- SEO safety
- internal-link safety
- medical/legal escalation triggers
- founder gate need

No article enters scoring until blocking QA issues are resolved or explicitly escalated.

## 10. Scoring/Rubric Workflow

Scoring follows `ARTICLE_SCORING_RUBRIC.md`.

Use scoring after initial QA.

Scoring output must include:

- total score
- category scores
- hard stops
- readiness state
- top revision priorities
- escalation need

Publish thresholds, revision thresholds, and escalation thresholds come from the rubric.

## 11. Founder Review Gates

Founder review is required when:

- the article is a pillar article
- the topic carries authority positioning
- article is David-facing and emotionally sensitive
- article is commerce-sensitive
- medical/legal escalation territory is present
- book, founder, author, or platform-as-second-edition language changes
- product, quiz, program, or affiliate framing changes
- reviewers are divided

Founder review packet:

- draft
- readiness state
- score, if scored
- open decision
- risk notes
- recommended action

Do not ask founder to discover unresolved workflow issues.

## 12. Revision Loops

Revision loops should be narrow.

Each revision request must state:

- issue
- location
- governing standard
- required change
- owner
- return state

Common revision routes:

- evidence issue: return to research
- claim issue: return to writer or research
- persona issue: return to editorial/brand
- SEO issue: return to SEO review
- internal-link issue: return to linking review
- commerce issue: return to commerce/firewall review
- founder decision: return to founder gate

After revision, rerun only affected checks unless the article changed substantially.

## 13. Publish-Ready Workflow

An article is `Publish Ready` only when:

- QA passes
- scoring threshold passes
- no hard stops remain
- required founder gate is complete
- citations and source links are verified
- internal links are planned and safe
- commerce and affiliate boundaries pass
- title, metadata, and headings are claim-safe
- CTA pathway is appropriate

`Publish Ready` means editorially ready. It does not define CMS implementation steps.

## 14. Update/Refresh Workflow

Refresh is required when:

- source freshness is weak
- source links break
- research changes
- governance changes
- article overlaps a newer article
- internal links need rerouting
- claim language no longer fits current standards
- article is strategically important and stale

Refresh workflow:

1. identify trigger
2. mark `Refresh Needed`
3. review research and citations
4. update claims and links
5. rerun affected QA checks
6. rescore if changes are material
7. route to founder only if required

Do not refresh only to look active.

## 15. Archival And Deprecation Workflow

Deprecate or archive when:

- article is no longer accurate
- article duplicates a stronger page
- topic is outside current scope
- claim territory is unsafe
- source support cannot be maintained
- article undermines brand or authority

Deprecation decision must state:

- reason
- replacement page, if any
- internal links to update
- whether content is merged, archived, or removed from active guidance

Do not leave deprecated pages in active reader pathways.

## 16. Ownership And Responsibilities

Ownership is by function, not title.

### Topic Owner

- defines reader need, topic role, and go/no-go recommendation

### SEO Owner

- handles keyword intake, search intent, cluster role, metadata safety, and discovery pathway

### Research Owner

- builds research packet and validates claims

### Writer

- drafts within brief, evidence, persona, and brand constraints

### Editorial Reviewer

- checks structure, clarity, fit, and revision quality

### QA Reviewer

- runs `CONTENT_QA.md` checks and readiness state

### Scoring Reviewer

- applies `ARTICLE_SCORING_RUBRIC.md`

### Founder

- resolves judgment gates and final strategic/editorial decisions when required

One person may hold multiple roles. The responsibilities remain distinct.

## 17. Escalation Workflow

Escalate when:

- governance conflicts
- evidence is weak or conflicting
- medical/legal risk appears
- commerce pressure affects claims
- search opportunity requires unsafe wording
- internal-link pathway creates misleading authority flow
- founder decision is required
- readiness state is disputed

Escalation output:

```txt
ISSUE
<what is blocked>

LOCATION
<article / section / claim / link>

RISK
<evidence, legal, brand, persona, commerce, SEO, linking>

OPTIONS
<approve, hedge, revise, source, cut, defer, archive>

RECOMMENDATION
<one recommended action>
```

## 18. Workflow States And Transitions

Use these states consistently:

| State | Moves To |
|---|---|
| Idea | Candidate Topic, Deferred |
| Candidate Topic | Approved Topic, Deferred, Rejected |
| Approved Topic | Research Needed, Brief Ready |
| Research Needed | Research Packet Ready, Escalation Needed |
| Research Packet Ready | Brief Ready |
| Brief Ready | Drafting |
| Drafting | Editorial Review |
| Editorial Review | QA Review, Revision Needed |
| QA Review | Scoring, Revision Needed, Escalation Needed |
| Scoring | Founder Review Ready, Revision Needed, Publish Ready, Escalation Needed |
| Founder Review Ready | Revision Needed, Publish Ready, Deferred |
| Publish Ready | Published |
| Published | Refresh Needed, Deprecated |
| Refresh Needed | QA Review, Published, Deprecated |
| Deprecated | Archived |

Do not skip from Drafting to Publish Ready.

## 19. Operational Defaults

When uncertain:

- narrow the topic
- clarify the reader
- validate the claim
- cite the source
- protect the brand
- separate evidence from commerce
- choose fewer links
- escalate early
- ask founder only for founder-level decisions

The workflow is successful when StrongPath can publish serious work quickly with low rework and low risk.
