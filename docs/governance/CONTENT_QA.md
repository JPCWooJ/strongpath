# Content QA

## Purpose

- Define the minimum QA pass before StrongPath content publishes.
- Verify claims, citations, persona, brand, SEO, links, commerce, and escalation needs.
- Keep founder review focused on founder-level judgment.

## Core Rules

- QA protects reader trust.
- QA does not rewrite from scratch; it verifies and routes.
- If a claim cannot be verified, source it, hedge it, rewrite it, cut it, or escalate it.
- Do not send known QA defects to founder review.
- Founder is not QA.

## Required Workflow

- Confirm brief or article purpose.
- Verify claims.
- Verify citations and links.
- Check persona fit.
- Check brand/tone.
- Check commerce firewall.
- Check SEO safety.
- Check internal links.
- Check medical/legal escalation triggers.
- Assign readiness state.

## Claim QA

- Claim is directly supported.
- Claim is no stronger than source.
- Quantitative claims have exact support.
- Safety claims use clinical or institutional support.
- Mechanism claims are accurate and plain.
- Landmark older studies are labeled as older.
- Current-practice claims use current sources.

## Citation QA

- Major claims have citations.
- Source link resolves.
- Source title, institution/author, and date are identifiable.
- Source supports the exact claim.
- Citation is near the claim.
- Source list exists when required.
- Book is not used as proof for current health claims.
- Affiliate links are separated from evidence links.

## Persona / Brand QA

- Primary reader is clear.
- Margaret copy is peer-level, never patronizing.
- David copy is goodwill-led, not fear-led.
- Clinician-visible copy is citation-visible and bounded.
- Tone is serious, direct, hopeful.
- No hype, generic wellness filler, anti-aging frame, or forbidden promise language.
- Metadata and headings do not overclaim.

## Commerce QA

- Product, quiz, program, affiliate, and book claims are narrower than research claims.
- Commerce appears after education.
- Affiliate disclosure exists where required.
- Evidence links and purchase links are separate.
- Product language does not imply diagnosis, treatment, prevention, reversal, clinical outcome, or guaranteed result.

## SEO / Linking QA

- Keyword does not force unsafe wording.
- "Prevent" and disease-adjacent terms are education-safe.
- "Reverse" is not used in StrongPath voice.
- Metadata matches body claims.
- Anchor text is honest and claim-safe.
- Internal links create a useful reader path.
- No important page is orphaned when publishing.

## Readiness States

- Draft: not QA complete.
- Research Needed: source support is incomplete.
- Revision Needed: copy, claim, persona, brand, SEO, link, or commerce issue remains.
- Escalation Needed: medical, legal, governance, brand, or founder-level risk remains.
- Founder Review Ready: QA passed; founder decision is the next gate.
- Publish Ready: no blockers remain.
- Deferred: topic or claim is not currently safe or useful.

## Pass / Fail

Pass when:

- claims, citations, persona, brand, SEO, links, commerce, and safety all pass
- required founder gate is resolved
- article is ready for publishing workflow

Fail when:

- any substantive claim is unsupported
- source links fail
- metadata or headings overclaim
- commerce borrows evidence authority
- persona or brand posture drifts
- escalation trigger is unresolved

## Escalate

- Disease treatment, prevention, reversal, recovery, fall-prevention, supplement, frailty, post-fall, post-surgery, hospitalization, contraindication, or medically complex adult content.
- Product or affiliate claims adjacent to health outcomes.
- Implied clinician, scientist, medical advisor, or author involvement beyond governance.
- Reviewer disagreement on risk.
- Founder, book, product, quiz, program, or platform framing changes.
