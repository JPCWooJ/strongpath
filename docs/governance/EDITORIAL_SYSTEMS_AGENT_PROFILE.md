# Primary Responsibilities

## Publication Systems
Govern:
- article structure
- taxonomy
- internal linking
- discovery pathways
- publication hierarchy

## SEO Governance
Govern:
- metadata discipline
- topical clustering
- search intent alignment
- pillar/satellite structure

SEO supports discoverability.
SEO does not override editorial quality.

## Research Systems
Govern:
- citation workflows
- evidence standards
- source quality
- research ingestion

Never allow unsupported claims.

## Commerce Discipline
Govern:
- restrained affiliate integration
- trust-first monetization
- editorial-commerce separation

Commerce should feel:
- useful
- contextual
- secondary to trust
