# Article Scoring Rubric

## Purpose

- Give reviewers a consistent publish-readiness score.
- Identify blockers, revision priorities, and escalation needs.
- Make judgment faster, not heavier.

## Core Rules

- Score the article the reader experiences.
- Governance wins over score.
- A high score cannot override a hard stop.
- Use whole numbers.
- Score after basic QA, not before.

## Scoring Model

- Evidence quality: 15
- Citation discipline: 15
- Persona alignment: 10
- Brand/tone: 10
- SEO quality: 10
- Internal linking: 10
- Commerce firewall: 10
- Readability/usability: 10
- Authority-building: 10
- Total: 100

## Category Standards

- Evidence: claims are supported by appropriate, current, direct sources.
- Citation: sources are visible, close to claims, correctly used, and commerce-separated.
- Persona: reader, moment, objections, and next step are clear.
- Brand/tone: serious, direct, hopeful; specialist not salesman.
- SEO: intent, title, metadata, headings, URL, and keywords are useful and claim-safe.
- Internal linking: pathway is clear, anchors are honest, no orphan risk.
- Commerce firewall: product, affiliate, quiz, book, and evidence claims are separated.
- Readability: article is scannable, specific, and easy to use.
- Authority-building: article strengthens cluster, citation record, brand trust, or safe pathway.

## Score Bands

- 90-100: strong publish candidate.
- 85-89: publish candidate after minor fixes or required gate.
- 75-84: targeted revision needed.
- 60-74: major revision or research needed.
- Below 60: rebuild, defer, or rewrite brief.

Pillar articles should normally score 90+.

## Publish Threshold

Publish ready requires:

- total score 85+
- no hard stops
- evidence 12+
- citation 12+
- commerce firewall 8+
- required founder gate resolved
- all escalations resolved

## Hard Stops

- Major health claim lacks support.
- Book used as proof for current health claim.
- Product language implies diagnosis, treatment, prevention, reversal, clinical outcome, or guarantee.
- "Reverse" or "reversing" appears outside the book title as a StrongPath claim.
- Category score of 0.
- Unresolved medical/legal/commerce escalation.

## Pass / Fail

Pass when:

- threshold is met
- hard stops are absent
- scoring explains the decision
- next action is clear

Fail when:

- score hides unresolved QA problems
- reviewer rewards effort, length, novelty, or taste over governance
- revision priorities are vague
- founder is asked to discover defects

## Escalate

- Hard stop appears.
- Evidence or citation score below 10 on health content.
- Commerce firewall below 8 on commerce-sensitive content.
- SEO score below 6 because of unsafe keyword pressure.
- Reviewers differ by 10+ points.
- New founder, book, author, product, quiz, or program framing appears.
