# Topic Cluster Architecture

## Purpose

- Define how StrongPath organizes topics into pillars, satellites, and pathways.
- Build topical authority through depth, clarity, restraint, and maintenance.
- Prevent keyword sprawl, duplicate pages, and unclear reader journeys.

## Core Rules

- A cluster is an answer system, not a keyword bucket.
- Every article needs a role, reader, intent, evidence boundary, and internal-link path.
- Depth before breadth.
- Refresh or strengthen pillars before expanding clusters.
- If two pages answer the same reader in the same way, merge or narrow.

## Canonical Cluster Families

- Sarcopenia and age-related muscle loss.
- Strength as independence.
- Resistance training execution.
- Protein, recovery, and progression.
- Caregiver support and parent-strength guidance.
- Book and brand-term defense.
- Safety, late-start, and older-adult adaptation.

## Cluster Requirements

- clear reader problem
- primary persona or persona mix
- durable boundary
- parent pillar or planned pillar
- satellite roles
- search-intent map
- evidence requirements
- internal-link pathway
- commerce and book boundaries
- refresh expectation

## Pillars

Pillars must:

- answer the broad topic clearly
- define terms and boundaries
- cite deeply enough for risk
- link to useful satellites
- separate education from commerce
- establish the cluster pathway
- avoid search-driven overclaims

## Satellites

Satellites must:

- answer one focused question
- stay narrower than the pillar
- link to the parent pillar
- avoid duplicating siblings
- cite according to risk
- provide one useful next step

## Expansion Gate

Expand when:

- pillar is current
- reader need is real
- intent is distinct
- evidence can support claims
- internal links can route clearly
- page strengthens authority

Do not expand for keyword variants, thin topics, unsafe claims, or premature commerce.

## Overlap Review

Before approving a new article, check:

- existing pages
- planned pages
- search intent
- persona moment
- claim territory
- internal-link role

Overlap is not solved by changing the title.

## Cluster States

- Candidate: validate reader need and evidence.
- Seed: initial pillar or key article exists.
- Emerging: pillar plus useful satellites.
- Mature: coherent, current, internally linked.
- Refresh Needed: claims, links, or structure are stale.
- Merge/Split Needed: boundary or intent is unclear.
- Deprecated: no longer active strategy.

## Merge / Split

Merge when:

- pages serve same reader problem
- pillars compete for same intent
- one cluster is too thin
- links confuse authority flow

Split when:

- one pillar serves multiple major intents
- personas need different pathways
- evidence or safety rules differ sharply
- commerce-sensitive content needs separation

## Pass / Fail

Pass when:

- cluster has clear boundary, role map, links, evidence expectations, and maintenance path
- new content fills a distinct need
- authority becomes clearer

Fail when:

- topic is only a keyword bucket
- pages duplicate intent
- pillar is stale but cluster keeps expanding
- internal links cannot connect honestly
- commerce pressure drives topic creation

## Escalate

- Boundary dispute.
- Pillar claim territory is risky.
- New topic overlaps active content.
- Merge/split affects a pillar.
- Cluster targets crisis, clinical, or out-of-scope intent.
- Founder, book, author, product, quiz, or program framing changes.
