# Editorial Art Direction System

## Purpose

- Govern StrongPath article presentation.
- Keep articles readable, credible, modern, and publication-grade.
- Use proven publication patterns before custom invention.

## Core Rules

- Template first: Tailwind Typography for prose; Tailwind Next.js Starter Blog for article/listing structure.
- Ghost and Astro Paper are references, not systems to clone.
- No custom SVG, abstract art systems, or synthetic diagrams unless explicitly approved.
- Mobile readability is required.
- Founder is not QA; Codex/agents must verify before delivery.

## Hero

- Use metadata, headline, concise deck, and one strong visual when useful.
- Prefer professional editorial photos or clean image treatment.
- Avoid fear imagery, cheesy senior stock, wellness cliches, and medical dramatization.
- Keep title wrapping controlled on mobile.
- Do not let hero design imply stronger claims than the article makes.

## Prose Rhythm

- Use narrow readable body columns.
- Use Tailwind Typography or equivalent prose defaults.
- Keep section gaps continuous: no oversized dead space.
- Headings should be direct, short, and useful.
- Lists should clarify, not decorate.
- Avoid long uninterrupted text blocks in flagship articles.

## Imagery

- Use imagery only when it improves trust, comprehension, or reader momentum.
- Prefer functional movement, dignified active aging, real objects, or relevant editorial context.
- Store required local article images in `public/images/articles/`.
- Record source/credit when using third-party open-source imagery.
- Do not use copyrighted reference publication imagery.

## Modules

- Use restrained modules only:
  - key takeaways
  - evidence/source box
  - practical checklist
  - safety note
  - related reading
  - contextual CTA
- Each module must do a different job.
- Do not stack components to create fake depth.
- Evidence modules must stay source-adjacent and claim-safe.

## Mobile

- Treat mobile as the baseline.
- Verify title, deck, images, tags, modules, CTAs, and sources do not collide or wrap awkwardly.
- Buttons and links must remain tappable.
- If a visual only works on desktop, cut it or simplify it.

## Color And Contrast

- Use parchment as the reading base.
- Use navy, verdigris, and restrained gold for hierarchy.
- Avoid beige-only pages, black/beige monotony, low contrast, decorative gradients, and color without a job.
- Text contrast wins over mood.

## CTA

- CTA hierarchy:
  - related article or cluster path
  - book page when contextually relevant
  - waitlist/email only when useful
- Keep CTAs quiet and pathway-based.
- Do not commercialize evidence sections.

## Stop-Loss

- After two failed visual iterations, stop refining the custom approach.
- Switch to the proven publication baseline or rollback.
