# SEO Systems

## Purpose

- Govern StrongPath search visibility without weakening trust.
- Make topics discoverable while preserving evidence, brand, persona, citation, and commerce discipline.
- Keep SEO useful, narrow, and non-spammy.

## Core Rules

- Search demand shapes the doorway; evidence and brand govern the room.
- One article needs one primary intent.
- No content farms, keyword stuffing, duplicate pages, or unsafe claim chasing.
- SEO cannot override citation, QA, persona, or brand rules.
- If ranking requires unsafe language, do not target the keyword.

## Topic Roles

- Pillar: broad authority page for a cluster.
- Satellite: focused support article for one narrower question.
- FAQ: short answer to a specific question.
- Book/authority page: brand and credibility defense.
- Product/support page: commerce-sensitive education or pathway.
- Tool/support asset: practical support with clear claim limits.

## Canonical Cluster Families

- Sarcopenia and age-related muscle loss.
- Strength as independence.
- Resistance training execution.
- Protein, recovery, and progression.
- Caregiver support and parent-strength guidance.
- Book and brand-term defense.
- Safety, late-start, and older-adult adaptation.

## Search Intent

- Informational: explain.
- Executional: show what to do.
- Safety: clarify risk and limits.
- Caregiver: help someone support a parent.
- Commercial-adjacent: educate before purchase.
- Navigational: StrongPath or book lookup.

Answer intent early. Do not turn informational pages into sales pages.

## Metadata

- Title uses the primary topic naturally.
- Description states the reader problem and evidence-safe promise.
- OpenGraph and social metadata follow the same claim discipline.
- Metadata cannot claim more than the page body.
- Avoid fear, hype, disease promises, and clickbait.

## URL Rules

- Lowercase hyphenated slugs.
- Short, stable, durable topic.
- No dates unless core to the asset.
- No hype words or medical promises.
- Do not create multiple URLs for the same intent.
- URL changes require governance review.

## Keyword Rules

- Use persona language over awkward exact match.
- Use secondary terms only when they serve the reader.
- Do not echo unsafe "reverse," "cure," "anti-aging," "hack," or disease-prevention phrasing.
- Use "senior" or "elderly" only where `PERSONAS.md` permits.
- Excluded keywords remain excluded unless governance changes.

## Article Hierarchy

- H1 states the topic plainly.
- Opener answers why the article matters.
- Early section defines the problem or answer.
- Evidence appears before strong recommendations.
- Safety limits appear before action steps when risk exists.
- Commerce appears after education.
- Conclusion routes to one useful next step.

## Internal Linking

- Satellites link to parent pillars.
- Pillars link to the most useful satellites.
- Anchor text is honest, specific, and claim-safe.
- Book links support credibility, not proof.
- Evidence, internal, and commerce links stay distinct.

## Pass / Fail

Pass when:

- article has clear role, intent, persona, keyword, metadata, URL, and links
- search structure helps the reader
- claims stay evidence-safe
- no duplicate or thin page is created

Fail when:

- SEO pushes unsafe wording
- metadata overclaims
- page duplicates another intent
- article exists only for keyword variants
- commerce pressure drives the page before education

## Escalate

- Unsafe keyword opportunity.
- Crisis, clinical, or out-of-scope intent.
- Proposed URL change.
- Overlap with existing content.
- Affiliate, supplement, product, quiz, or program pressure.
- Metadata would need to overclaim.
- AI-search tactic creates thin or duplicate content.
