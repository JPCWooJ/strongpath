## Article Summary

```txt
Sarcopenia is age-related loss of muscle strength, muscle quantity, and physical function. The article explains the definition, early signs, why it matters for independence, and what the evidence supports: progressive resistance training, adequate protein, recovery, and appropriate clinical guidance when needed.
```
