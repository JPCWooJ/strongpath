# Article Launch Package

## Purpose

Define the repeatable workflow for moving one StrongPath article from approved topic to one reviewable launch package.

Goal: agents prepare the package; founder approves only defined exceptions.

## 1. When To Use

Use for every article moving into publication, newsletter, X, search, commerce, or KPI execution.

Do not use for:

- broad strategy
- governance updates
- site redesign
- article copy rewrites outside the approved draft process

## 2. Required Inputs

- approved topic or article brief
- primary persona and intent
- target article slug
- draft or draft source
- sources/citations
- CTA target
- related articles/internal links
- commerce relevance, if any
- target publish window

## 3. Output Package Location

Create one package per article in:

`docs/growth/launch-packages/`

Filename:

`<article-slug>-launch-package.md`

## 4. Workflow Stages

1. Topic approved: confirm weekly priority, persona, intent, and owner.
2. Article brief: define angle, source needs, CTA, links, and risk.
3. Draft: prepare article through normal editorial process.
4. Editorial/source/claim QA: verify sourcing, claims, disclaimer/disclosure needs, and brand voice.
5. Google Search readiness: apply `docs/governance/GOOGLE_SEARCH_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md` and `docs/growth/STRONGPATH_GOOGLE_SEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md`.
6. CMS publishing package: prepare title, description, slug, canonical, OG/social metadata, excerpt, and publish status; exclude public read-time display.
7. Internal linking: define inbound and outbound article links.
8. Amazon/e-commerce module: recommend none, book, quiz, program, or product path; include disclosure requirement.
9. X distribution package: apply `docs/governance/X_ALGORITHM_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md` and `docs/growth/STRONGPATH_X_DISTRIBUTION_PLAYBOOK.md`.
10. Beehiiv package: prepare subject, preview text, summary, CTA, and send timing.
11. Google Business Profile recommendation: recommend post, no post, or hold.
12. KPI tracking row: create the measurement row before publish.
13. 7-day review: record performance, issues, and next action.

## 5. Founder Approval Gates

Founder approval required only for:

- weekly priority approval
- sensitive claim approval
- final article approval when needed
- major strategy exception

Founder approval not required for:

- metadata preparation
- internal-link recommendations
- X package preparation
- Beehiiv draft preparation
- KPI row creation
- execution of approved patterns

## 6. Required Launch Package Contents

Each package includes:

- article metadata
- Google Search readiness
- internal links
- commerce module recommendation
- X package
- Beehiiv package
- Google Business Profile recommendation
- KPI tracking row
- unresolved issues

Required checks:

- Google Search readiness
- StrongPath Google Search Playbook
- internal links
- Amazon affiliate module
- X distribution handoff
- Beehiiv handoff
- KPI row / measurement

## 7. KPI Row

Create a row with:

- article slug
- publish date
- primary persona
- topic cluster
- acquisition source
- sessions
- organic impressions/clicks/CTR
- newsletter signups
- qualified X clicks
- saves/bookmarks
- credible replies
- quiz starts
- affiliate clicks, if applicable
- 7-day decision: promote, refresh, reframe, hold, or escalate

## 8. Standard Deliverable Format

```txt
ARTICLE
<title + slug>

STATE
<topic approved / draft / QA / ready to publish / published / 7-day review>

OWNER
<current owner>

APPROVAL
<none / founder / sensitive claim / strategy exception>

PACKAGE
<path in docs/growth/launch-packages/>

READY
<yes/no>

UNRESOLVED ISSUES
<none or bullets>

NEXT ACTION
<single next step>
```
