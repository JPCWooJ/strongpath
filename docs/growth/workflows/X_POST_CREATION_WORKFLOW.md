# X Post Creation Workflow

## Purpose

Define how StrongPath turns launch-package strategy into public X copy.

Goal: public copy sounds like a serious human editor, not an internal planning note.

## 1. When To Use

Use before any StrongPath article post, no-link post, reply-seeding post, or founder amplification is handed to Jeff for posting.

Do not use for:

- algorithm strategy
- topic selection
- article editing
- Postiz scheduling mechanics

## 2. GTM Input

GTM owns distribution intent:

- post / reply / wait
- link / no link
- objective
- audience
- article
- topic lane
- KPI
- timing
- reply-search target, if any

GTM does not finalize public X copy unless explicitly requested.

## 3. Editorial Output

Editorial owns final public copy:

- voice
- clarity
- rhythm
- claim safety
- persona fit
- human specificity
- paste-ready wording

Editorial produces one strong post before multiple weak options.

## 4. Founder Output

Founder receives:

- final paste-ready copy
- account: `@StrongPathHQ` or Jeff
- link placement
- timing recommendation
- one reason the post is ready

Founder should not receive internal notes as public copy.

## 5. Quality Bar

Public X copy must:

- sound like a serious human editor
- stand alone without the click
- use concrete human examples
- make one clear point
- use plain sentences
- preserve StrongPath voice: serious, direct, hopeful
- stay inside article evidence

Prefer:

- concrete observation
- practical meaning
- article link if link post

## 6. Prohibited Copy Patterns

Do not use:

- stiff agent phrasing
- internal strategy language
- awkward abstractions
- repeated formulas from prior posts
- vague "daily life" language without examples
- generic wellness language
- sales language
- hype
- fear
- fall-prevention claims
- medical claims

Avoid phrases like:

- "strength becomes usable"
- "the nervous system organizes movement"
- "daily capacity" unless grounded in stairs, reaching, carrying, getting up, or another human example

## 7. Pass / Fail Examples

FAIL:

> Balance and mobility after 50 are not separate from strength. They are how strength becomes usable in daily life.

Why: abstract, internal, stiff, and over-formulaic.

FAIL:

> The foot, ankle, hip, trunk, and nervous system still have to organize the movement.

Why: too technical and unnatural for X.

PASS pattern:

> Stairs, low chairs, curbs, and reaching overhead all ask for more than strength alone.
>
> Balance and mobility matter because they help ordinary movement feel steadier.
>
> <article link>

## 8. Acceptance Test

Before founder handoff:

- GTM intent is clear
- one audience is primary
- post sounds human when read aloud
- first line is concrete
- link post is useful without the click
- no banned claim or phrase
- no internal planning language
- founder receives paste-ready copy only
