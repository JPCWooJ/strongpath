# StrongPath Google Search Playbook

## Purpose

Define how Google Search supports StrongPath GTM.

This file is a task-specific reference for search-to-conversion work. It is not daily GTM first-read material.

Use it when GTM needs to evaluate search demand, organic landing-page behavior, email capture from organic traffic, or commerce paths from search.

## Classification

Classification: Task-specific reference.

Do not use this file to generate X posts, Beehiiv copy, founder amplification, or article launch copy.

Search intent may inform a distribution angle, but search language must not become social copy.

## 1. Approved Topic Clusters

Phase 1 clusters:

- sarcopenia / age-related muscle loss
- strength as independence
- resistance training execution
- protein and recovery
- starting strength training after 60
- helping aging parents stay strong
- evidence that older adults can regain strength
- StrongPath book / brand-term defense

Rules:

- stay inside approved clusters unless founder approves expansion
- prioritize Margaret and David intent
- keep Dr. Chen / clinician targeting Phase 2 unless found organically
- avoid anti-aging, miracle, hack, cure, reversal, and fear-led queries
- defer acute medical, post-surgery, and crisis-care queries without qualified review

## 2. Search-To-Email / Commerce Paths

Email paths:

- informational article -> quiz or newsletter capture
- placeholder/future article -> notify-me capture
- assessment/tool page -> email capture before or after result
- caregiver article -> caregiver-track capture

Commerce paths:

- book-intent page -> book affiliate link
- execution article -> quiz, program, or book path
- product-adjacent article -> disclosure first, education before commerce
- no commerce CTA may make claims stronger than the article evidence

Rules:

- do not force aggressive funnels
- match CTA to reader readiness
- preserve editorial trust before conversion

## 3. Search Console Review Loop

Weekly review:

- indexed pages / indexing errors
- top organic landing pages
- organic impressions, clicks, CTR
- high-impression / low-CTR pages
- pages discovered but not indexed
- query drift outside approved clusters

Monthly review:

- cluster performance
- internal-link gaps
- refresh candidates
- consolidation candidates
- email and commerce conversion from organic landing pages

## Owner Model

Editorial Systems owns:

- search intent fit
- article readiness
- source quality
- internal links
- refresh or consolidation recommendations

GTM owns:

- Search Console readout
- organic source quality
- email and commerce path review
- conversion learning from organic landing pages

CTO owns:

- crawlability
- sitemap
- metadata implementation
- canonicals / redirects / noindex
- tracking reliability

Founder owns:

- new topic clusters
- sensitive claims
- major positioning shifts
- high-risk commerce or medical judgment
